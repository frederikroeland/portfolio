The challenge

A global finance group needed to adopt IFRS 9 consistently across more than twenty countries, each with local systems, data and reporting practices. Classification and measurement, expected-credit-loss impairment and FINREP reporting all had to land on a common foundation while respecting local nuance.

The approach

As practice lead and programme manager, I established a single implementation playbook of standardised methods, quality gates and reusable delivery assets, so that every country team worked from the same blueprint. I aligned classification, impairment and FINREP requirements to shared product capabilities and coordinated delivery across the region.

The outcome

The programme delivered a consistent IFRS 9 capability across 20+ countries, with reusable implementation standards that were adopted region-wide and accelerated later rollouts. The resulting capability was managed and operated centrally.
