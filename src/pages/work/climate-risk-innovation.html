The challenge

Banks faced fast-moving expectations on climate risk, from supervisors, investors and their own boards, but lacked a clear way to quantify and communicate the impact of climate factors on their portfolios.

The approach

As global product director, I shaped the vision and value proposition for a climate-risk impact analysis capability. I translated emerging ESG regulation and supervisory guidance into concrete product requirements, and worked across the organisation to bring the capability to market.

The outcome

The climate-risk impact analysis capability was recognised with a global innovation award, giving financial-institution clients a credible, structured way to assess climate-related impact.
